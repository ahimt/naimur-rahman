--------------------------------------------------
HOW TO PRINT / SAVE AS PDF:
--------------------------------------------------

1. Open "page1.html" in your browser.
2. Press Ctrl + P (or Cmd + P on Mac) to open the print dialog.
3. Destination: Select "Save as PDF".
4. Paper Size: Choose "A4".
5. Margins: Set to "None" or "Default".
6. Check "Background graphics" if your browser prompts.
7. Click Save to generate a high-resolution, print-perfect PDF.
